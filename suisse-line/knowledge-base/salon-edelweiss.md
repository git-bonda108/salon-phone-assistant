# Salon Edelweiss – Knowledge base for the phone assistant

## About
Salon Edelweiss is a hair salon in Zürich-Seefeld for women, men and children. Team: Lara (owner, colour specialist), Marco (barber), Sina (stylist).

## Address and getting here
- Seefeldstrasse 42, 8008 Zürich
- Tram 2 or 4 to "Feldeggstrasse", 2 minutes on foot.
- Blue-zone street parking nearby; public car park "Opera" about 7 minutes on foot.
- The salon is on the ground floor and wheelchair accessible.

## Opening hours
- Monday: closed
- Tuesday, Wednesday, Friday: 09:00–18:30
- Thursday: 09:00–20:00 (late opening)
- Saturday: 08:00–16:00
- Sunday and public holidays (canton Zürich): closed

Not offered by phone booking (consultation first, Lara calls back): colour corrections, hair extensions, perms, bridal packages.

## Policies
- Please arrive 5 minutes early.
- Cancellation or moving is free up to 24 hours before the appointment. Later cancellations or no-shows may be charged 50% of the service price.
- First colour appointment: a free 5-minute allergy patch test is recommended at least 48 hours before. It can be done any time during opening hours without an appointment.
- Children under 12 must be accompanied by an adult.
- Late arrival of more than 15 minutes: the appointment may need to be shortened or moved.

## Payment
TWINT, Maestro/debit cards, Visa, Mastercard and cash. Gift vouchers are available in the salon in any amount and are valid for 2 years.
